Your email & privacy

By clicking Join waitlist, you ask Forehead to email you when it launches. We store your email address and the time of that request in Supabase. The list is not public.

Your ad text and handle stay in this browser tab. They are included in your downloaded or shared image. We remember that you joined in this tab so you can return to your ad; your email is not saved in browser storage.
